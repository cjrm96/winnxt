A WINNXT Studios tool / Quickstart

Crisis Triage
Rapid Response

01 / OPEN IT

Double-click the file

It opens in whatever browser you already use. There is nothing to install, no account, and no subscription. It works with your internet switched off.

  • Use a computer. Windows, Mac or Chromebook. Phones handle downloaded files awkwardly and this is not worth fighting with at the moment you need it.
  • Keep the file somewhere you will find it. Your desktop is fine. You want it before you need it, not during.

02 / ANSWER IT

Ten questions, one at a time

About five minutes. It asks whether this is political or business first, then works through what happened, where it surfaced, how far it has travelled, and how much damage it actually does.

  • Answer honestly rather than hopefully. Two questions decide most of the read: is it true, and does it actually damage you. Wishful answers produce a confident read of a situation you are not in.
  • You can change anything later. Every answer has a Change link on the final page.

03 / USE THE READ

The verdict comes first

The top of the report tells you whether to respond at all. Everything under it explains that call: the risk level, where to answer, the research behind it, and people who faced the same decision.

  • Fill in the draft fields. A holding line, a statement, and a longer version assemble from your own words as you type.
  • The draft is deliberately flat. Put it in your own voice before it goes anywhere.

04 / KEEP IT

Nothing is saved

Not to a server, not to your browser, not to a file. That is on purpose: a candidate typing their worst moment into a shared computer should not leave it behind.

  • Print before you close the tab. Print, or Save as PDF from the print dialog, gives you the whole read including the case histories.
  • Closing the tab erases it. Re-running it takes five minutes, and the right answer changes as a story spreads anyway.

THE FIVE CALLS

What the verdict can tell you

Say nothing

It is false and it does no real damage. Responding is the only thing that would give it an audience.

Do not publish yet

Almost nobody has seen it. Write the response, keep it in your pocket, watch for it to spread.

Get ahead of it

True, damaging, and still contained. Tell it yourself, in full, before somebody tells it for you.

Answer if asked

True but not damaging. Confirm it without flinching and move on. No statement needed.

Respond today

Damaging and widely seen. Silence now reads as confirmation. Short and accurate first.

Stop using this tool and call someone if any of these are true. Somebody is unsafe, or being threatened or harassed. There is a question of defamation, or a lawyer has been mentioned. A regulator, a prosecutor, or an insurer is involved. This is a communications tool. It cannot assess legal risk or physical risk, and it will tell you the same thing.
