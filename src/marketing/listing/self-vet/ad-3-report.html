How it opens

Who else
knows.

At the top, vetting is a team of lawyers, your tax returns, and a session that keeps going until the answers stop changing. This is the basic version of that room.

  • MoneyBankruptcies, liens, unpaid payroll, campaign spending.
  • LegalArrests, settlements, allegations, anything filed about you.
  • SaidDeleted accounts, old jokes, intimate messages, hot mics.
  • RecordEvery claim on your bio that somebody could check.

Seven sections. Most people flag two or three per section, and are surprised by one.
